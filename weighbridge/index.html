Bridge simulator

Drive a vehicle on the bridge, wait for STABLE, then press CAPTURE. With a real indicator connected, this part is replaced by the live reading.

How a truck is weighed

  1. Loaded truck comes on the bridge. Fill vehicle and driver details, CAPTURE, press Save 1st weight. The ticket waits as pending.
  2. After unloading, the empty truck comes back. Pick its ticket from Waiting for 2nd weight, CAPTURE, press Save 2nd weight.
  3. Gross, tare and net (load without vehicle) are worked out and the slip is ready. It also works the other way round: empty first, loaded later.
  4. If the vehicle's empty weight is already on file, use Stored tare and finish in one trip.
  5. For a car or bus that only needs its own weight, use Single weighing.
