AI Controls

Phase 1 runs on a built-in rule engine. The AI model plugs into these same hooks in Phase 2.

Roadmap

LiveAuto category on add

Rule engine reads the product name and files it into one of 18 aisles. AI will handle names it has never seen.

LiveAuto stock remaining

Every sale, stock-in and stock-out updates the count and writes to the movement log.

LiveBarcode and QR scanning

USB scanner, phone camera, printable shelf labels, and a QR on every bill for reprints and returns.

Phase 2Reorder prediction

Forecast when each item runs out from its sales speed and suggest order quantities.

Phase 2Photo and voice add

Snap a product or say its name; AI fills name, category and barcode.

Phase 2Expiry and price watch

Warn on near-expiry batches and suggest discounts to clear them.

Phase 2Owner assistant

Ask "kal kitni sale hui?" or "kya khatam ho raha hai?" and get answers from the store data.
