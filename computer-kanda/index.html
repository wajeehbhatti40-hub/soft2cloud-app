Weighing platform

Put goods on the pan. Items heavier than the capacity trigger the overload alarm.

How to weigh and sell

Put goods on the pan, then type the price per kg (or press a hot key M1–M6, or type a PLU number and press PLU). Total appears instantly on both displays. Press M+ to add the item to the bill, repeat for other items, type the cash received and press CHG for change, then PRINT. For a container: put it on, press TARE, then add the goods. To count pieces: press MODE until PCS, put sample pieces on, type how many and press SAVE.

Feature list

Weighing

  • 9 capacities from 30 kg to 2000 kg, each with its own division (5 g to 500 g)
  • Stability detection with settling motion and STABLE lamp
  • Zero within ±4% of capacity, zero-centre lamp
  • Tare by weighing, plus preset tare typed on the keypad
  • Overload (-OL-) with buzzer and underload (-uL-) warning
  • Minimum load of 20 divisions enforced before selling
  • Data hold for moving loads such as live animals
  • Span calibration with a known test weight

Price computing

  • Unit price × weight computed live, rounded to rupees or paisa
  • 5 units: kg, g, lb, oz and man (40 kg); price converts with the unit
  • PLU memory with English and Urdu names, editable prices
  • Six hot keys M1–M6 for best-selling items
  • Fixed-price items (non-weighed, price × quantity)
  • Discount % and GST % on the bill

Billing

  • Accumulation (M+) of many items into one bill
  • Memory recall (MR), void last item, new customer
  • Change calculation from cash received
  • Printed receipt with shop name, number, date and time
  • Daily sales report with product totals and Z-report close

Working modes

  • Price computing for shops and sabzi mandi
  • Piece counting from a sample weight
  • Check-weighing with UNDER / OK / OVER lamps and limits

Displays and power

  • Dual display: front for seller, rear for customer, with thank-you message
  • Product name strip on both displays
  • Mains or rechargeable battery, charge level and blinking LO-BAT
  • Auto power-off on battery when idle
  • Backlight brightness in 4 steps, key beep on/off
